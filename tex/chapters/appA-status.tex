\chapter{Verification status}\label{app:status}
\small

This appendix records, for every formal result, how it was checked. ``Referee'' means an independent AI
referee that started without the authoring context, re-derived the proof and tried to break it;
``audited'' means a check by the agents that wrote the result (an audit pass or an exact checker), with no independent referee; ``cited'' means a known result from the literature. ``Lean'' means the statement, for rational numbers, is proved in Lean (Section~\ref{sec:lean-status}); these results were also refereed. Every computer check used exact
rational arithmetic (Python fractions) or the SMT solver z3 over linear real arithmetic with rational
inputs and one disjunction per complementarity condition, so an ``unsatisfiable'' answer covers every
equilibrium at once, including ties and boundary cases. No floating point was used except where stated.
The checks cover only small instances; for general sizes every result rests on its proof. All referees
belong to model families similar to the authors' and may share their blind spots. No human expert has
checked any part. The code column names checks in the accompanying \texttt{code/} folder (see its
\texttt{README.md} for commands and expected outputs); the Lean column names the theorem in the accompanying \texttt{lean/} project that machine-checks the
statement (Section~\ref{sec:lean-status}); ``---'' means not formalized.

\emph{Review rounds.} The results went through seven successive passes of AI referees, called rounds~1--3 and reviews~4--7; every referee started without the authoring context, and ``GPT'' marks passes run on a GPT-family model.

A third round of independent AI referees (GPT) gave the verdict ``correct with minor issues'' for the
material of Chapters~\ref{ch:market}, \ref{ch:arrival} and~\ref{ch:lck} and for the $\mathsf{UEOPL}$ and
subexponential results of Chapter~\ref{ch:upper}, and ``has errors'' for the P-LCP results (the P-LCP
violation type, now fixed). The minor fixes are applied in the text; no status entry below was raised.

\section{Lean formalization}\label{sec:lean-status}

The shorter proof of Appendix~\ref{app:short} is formalized in Lean~4 with Mathlib (about 7{,}700 lines).
The end-to-end theorem \texttt{main\_proof} states: for every ARRIVAL instance and every rational
Bayes--Nash equilibrium of the one-resource game built from it by the reduction of Appendix~\ref{app:short} (with one change: Lean's preprocessing keeps dead switches and redirects edges into them to \no{}, so $m=n$; the answer is preserved, \texttt{preprocess\_reaches\_yes\_proof}), the decoder returns the run's answer. A check in the project confirms that its
statement is exactly the specification, and \texttt{\#print axioms} reports only Lean's three standard
axioms (\texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice}, \texttt{Quot.sound}); no step is assumed. The
formalization includes a proof of Dohrau et al.'s Lemma~1~\cite{dgkmw17} and the flow-decomposition
facts used in Appendix~\ref{app:short}. It also proves, by an explicit construction, without Nash's theorem, that the constructed game has a rational Bayes--Nash equilibrium (\texttt{red\_bne\_exists\_proof}), so the end-to-end theorem is not vacuous, and that the constructed game is a legal instance of Definition~\ref{def:game} (priors form a distribution, payoffs ordered; \texttt{red\_wellFormed\_proof}). The size and bit-length bounds are also proved (every number has at most $28(n+1)$ bits and the whole game at most $8500(n+1)^5$ bits; \texttt{red\_bits}, \texttt{red\_descBits}). Not formalized: that $\Red$ and $\Dec$ run in polynomial time (Lean's reachability test \texttt{liveWithin} in the preprocessing is a specification whose own recursion can take time exponential in $n$; the project proves it equal to a stage-by-stage computation with $O(n^3)$ operations, \texttt{liveWithinFast\_eq}, and compiles it as that computation, so the executable preprocessing is polynomial, but Lean proves no running-time bound), the original construction of
Chapter~\ref{ch:arrival}, the game-to-market direction of Chapter~\ref{ch:market}, the subexponential algorithm of Chapter~\ref{ch:upper}, Chapter~\ref{ch:lck}, and what the next two paragraphs list as not formalized.
Lean certifies the theorem about the project's own definitions; their faithfulness to Conitzer's game,
to (M1) and to ARRIVAL was checked by an independent AI audit and should be read by a human
(the project's \texttt{DEFINITIONS.md} maps each definition to its source).

The direct reduction of Section~\ref{sec:direct-lcp} is formalized as well (about 2{,}100 further lines): for every well-formed game with $0<r<n$, $M+\delta I$ is a P-matrix and every solution of $\mathrm{LCP}(q,M+\delta I)$ yields, through the support-pattern LP, an exact BNE in marginal coordinates (\texttt{DirectLCP.\allowbreak{}main\_proof}; Lean builds the LCP with every type and forces the flows of zero-prior types to $0$, while the appendix drops those types first, so the two instances coincide when all priors are positive); for one resource, defender payoffs $0$ and $1$ and positive priors, marginal-coordinate BNE coincide with the BNE of Definition~\ref{def:game} (\texttt{DirectLCP.\allowbreak{}bneIffMarginal\_proof}). Not formalized there: existence of the P-LCP solution (a classical fact); the cases $r\in\{0,n\}$; restoring zero-prior types; the decoder to a mixed strategy over $r$-subsets and the link to Definition~\ref{def:game} for $r>1$; the bit length of $(q,M,\delta)$; and polynomial running time. An independent AI audit of these definitions against the appendix (Lean project, \texttt{audits/AUDIT-directlcp.md}) found them faithful; \texttt{DirectLCP/DEFINITIONS-directlcp.md} maps each definition to the appendix.

Three further parts are formalized (about 3{,}800 further lines, audited against the book in the Lean project's \texttt{audits/\allowbreak{}AUDIT-new-sets.md}, which found them faithful). \emph{Section~\ref{sec:plcp}:} the LCP formulation, the $P_0$ property, the diagonal perturbation, the uniform bound and gap, and Example~\ref{plcp:ex:negative} are proved as stated; for Theorem~\ref{plcp:thm:main}, Lean proves that $M+\varepsilon I$ is a P-matrix and that every solution of the perturbed LCP gives a feasible system~$(\ast)$ whose every point is an (M1) equilibrium (\texttt{MarketLCP.\allowbreak{}main\_proof}). \emph{Chapter~\ref{ch:market}, market to game:} Lemma~\ref{lem:mk-rescale} is proved as stated, and every BNE of $\Gamma_\omega$ decodes to a market equilibrium (\texttt{MarketGame.\allowbreak{}omegaCompose\_proof}); the lift is proved for the embedding of Appendix~\ref{app:short}, not for $\Gamma_\omega$. \emph{Section~\ref{sec:contraction}:} Lemma~\ref{lem:ueopl-pattern}(a) and~(c) (part~(b) is used inside the proof but not stated separately), Lemmas~\ref{lem:ueopl-proj}--\ref{lem:ueopl-budget}, the residual bound of Lemma~\ref{lem:ueopl-decoding}, and that every fixed point of $F$ decodes, through one pattern LP, to an exact BNE in marginal coordinates (\texttt{Contraction.\allowbreak{}reduction\_proof}). Not formalized there: existence of a solution of the perturbed LCP and of a rational fixed point of $F$ (both classical, and needed for the statements not to be vacuous), legality of $\Gamma_\omega$ and its prior bounds, polynomial running time and bit sizes, the UEOPL step through~\cite{fgms18}, the best-response condition for zero-prior types in the contraction's output, and the link from marginal coordinates to Definition~\ref{def:game} for $r>1$.

Lean names follow the source article's numbering: \texttt{fact0} is Fact~C.2, \texttt{lemma1}--\texttt{lemma6} are Lemmas~C.3--C.8, \texttt{theorem7} is Theorem~C.9 and \texttt{lemma8} is Lemma~C.10. The audits of the definitions are in the project's \texttt{audits/} folder.

\section{The implementation of the Li--Conitzer--Korzhyk algorithm}

All arithmetic is exact rational arithmetic. The paper's costs are logarithms of slopes and its rates
are $e^{-\mathrm{dist}}$; the implementation stores each path length as the exact rational
$e^{\mathrm{dist}}$, the product of the slope factors $b^{\pm1}$ along the path, so shortest paths,
negative-cycle tests and rates are exact. After every call to Algorithm~2, 3 or~4 the run asserts exactly
that the current state is an equilibrium for the budget equal to the current total coverage; no assertion
failed. The security-game reduction of~\cite[Section~3.1]{lck16} and an independent BNE checker written
directly in security-game payoffs were implemented separately. Validation: 40 random games (1--4 types,
2--5 targets, budgets $1$ to $(\text{targets})-1$, half with payoffs in $\{0,\dots,3\}$ to force ties),
160 runs under R1--R4: all outputs exact BNE, all per-step assertions held, Algorithm~4 exercised in most
runs, and the cycle-cancelling branch of Algorithm~2 cancelled at least one cycle in 11 runs.
